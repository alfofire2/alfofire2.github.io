APP PRIVACY

Your privacy isn't just a checkbox; it's our core principle. Our browser extension is designed to be invisible when it comes to your personal data.

PERSONAL DATA

WE DO NOT COLLECT, STORE, OR TRANSMIT ANY PERSONAL DATA. Period.

ANONYMOUS FUNCTIONALITY

To provide you with the best deals, the app only interacts with data anonymously:

  • COUPON SYNC: We fetch the public coupon list from our server. No user info is sent.
  • CURRENCY CONVERSION: We use public APIs to handle EUR/USD rates locally on your device.
  • CART READING: The app reads the total locally to suggest valid codes. This data never leaves your computer.
  • AFFILIATE LINKS: Support us by using our codes. This may open a background tab to register the referral.

WEBSITE PRIVACY

This website does not use tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or login systems. We simply don't know who you are, and we like it that way.
